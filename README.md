### A little about me

I'm a student developer from India. Most of what I know comes from building things, breaking them, and trying again.

I like working where **AI, product engineering, and design** meet. Sometimes that means experimenting with on-device AI. Other times, it's building a desktop app just because an idea sounds fun.

I care about the entire journey: **idea → prototype → actual people using it**.
